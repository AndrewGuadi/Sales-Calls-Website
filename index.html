Before you book.

How many guests can I invite?

Ask the venue to confirm capacity for your setup, especially if you need room for dancing, a buffet, or a dedicated photo area.

Can I bring my own caterer?

Ask about outside catering, kitchen access, and vendor requirements before hiring your caterer.

Can I add décor or linens?

Ask about renting linens, chair covers, backdrops, and centerpieces with your package.

How do I request a date?
